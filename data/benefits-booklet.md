# Maplewood Group Benefits Plan (sample booklet)

This is a fictional employee benefits booklet used to demonstrate a question-answering assistant. It does not describe any real insurance plan.

## Eligibility
Full-time employees working at least 30 hours per week are eligible on the first day of the month after 3 months of continuous employment. Part-time and contract employees are not eligible for group benefits.

## Enrolling dependents
You can cover your spouse or common-law partner and dependent children. Children are covered until age 21, or until age 25 if they are full-time students. Children with a disability that began before age 21 may stay covered at any age. Add dependents within 31 days of the birth, adoption or marriage; after that, proof of good health may be required.

## Dental care
Basic services such as cleanings, fillings and X-rays are reimbursed at 80%. Major services such as crowns and bridges are reimbursed at 50%. Orthodontics is not covered. Recall exams and cleanings are covered once every 9 months. The annual dental maximum is $1,500 per person.

## Vision care
Eyeglasses or contact lenses are covered up to $250 every 24 months per person. One eye exam is covered every 24 months for adults and every 12 months for children under 18. Laser eye surgery can be claimed against the same $250 vision maximum.

## Paramedical services
Massage therapy, physiotherapy, chiropractic care and psychology services are reimbursed at 80%, up to $500 per practitioner type per calendar year. Psychology services have a separate maximum of $1,500 per calendar year. A doctor's referral is not required. Receipts must show the practitioner's registration number.

## Prescription drugs
Eligible prescription drugs are reimbursed at 80% after a $5 dispensing fee deductible per prescription. Generic drugs are covered when available; if you choose a brand-name drug when a generic exists, you pay the difference. Some specialty drugs need prior authorization before the first fill.

## Employee and family assistance program
The employee and family assistance program (EFAP) offers free, confidential short-term counselling, available 24/7 by phone or online at no cost. It also covers legal, financial and nutrition advice. Using the EFAP does not reduce your paramedical psychology maximum.

## Travel emergency coverage
Emergency medical care outside your home province is covered up to $5,000,000 per trip for trips of up to 60 consecutive days. Call the 24-hour assistance line before receiving non-urgent treatment. Pre-existing conditions that were not stable in the 90 days before the trip are not covered.

## Life insurance
Basic life insurance pays two times your annual salary, to a maximum of $500,000. You can name or change your beneficiary at any time through the HR portal. Optional life insurance can be added in units of $10,000 with proof of good health.

## Short-term disability
Short-term disability pays 70% of your weekly earnings for up to 17 weeks, starting after a 7-day waiting period for illness. There is no waiting period for an accident or a hospital stay. A completed physician's statement is required within 30 days.

## Submitting a claim
Submit claims online through the member app by taking a photo of your receipt. Most online claims are paid by direct deposit within 5 business days. Paper claim forms are also accepted by mail and take up to 15 business days.

## Claim deadlines
Claims must be submitted within 12 months of the date the service was received. When your coverage ends, you have 90 days to submit claims for services received before the end date.

## Coordination of benefits
If your spouse also has group benefits, submit claims for yourself to your own plan first and claims for your spouse to their plan first. For children, submit first to the plan of the parent whose birthday comes earlier in the calendar year. The second plan can reimburse the remaining balance.

## Health spending account
Each employee receives a $400 health spending account every calendar year. It can pay for eligible health and dental expenses not covered by the plan, including amounts above a maximum. Unused balances carry forward for one year and are then forfeited.

## When coverage ends
Coverage ends on the last day of the month in which your employment ends, or when you retire. Life insurance can be converted to an individual policy within 31 days without proof of good health.
